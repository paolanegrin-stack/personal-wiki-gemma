Pan Europa Foods is a company seeking to increase its value rapidly and mitigate the risk of a hostile takeover. The board has a capital spending limit of 80 million ECU, while managers have proposed projects totaling 280 million ECU. The committee must select a combination of projects, not just approve every attractive proposal, using various criteria to make decisions.

## Key points

- The company aims to increase its value quickly and reduce the risk of a hostile takeover. (source: 02_Project_Selection_Methods_Summary.md, section 4)
- The board has capped capital spending at 80 million ECU, while managers have proposed projects costing 280 million ECU. (source: 02_Project_Selection_Methods_Summary.md, section 4)
- The committee must choose a combination of projects rather than simply approving every attractive proposal. (source: 02_Project_Selection_Methods_Summary.md, section 4)
- Ranking projects by factors such as fastest payback, highest NPV, or highest IRR can yield one result, while adding strategy, implementation difficulty, risk, project dependencies, and synergies can produce another. (source: 02_Project_Selection_Methods_Summary.md, section 4)
- Leaders from finance, distribution, production, sales, marketing, and strategy may value projects differently, and personal factors like ambitions and alliances can influence discussions. (source: 02_Project_Selection_Methods_Summary.md, section 4)
