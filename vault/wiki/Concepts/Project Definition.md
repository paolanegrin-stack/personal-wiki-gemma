A project is defined as temporary work that results in a unique product, service, or outcome, possessing both a start and an end. Effective project management is important because projects are often complex and uncertain, requiring management to assign responsibility, coordinate people, and turn goals into action.

## Key points

- A project is temporary work that creates a unique product, service, or result and has a beginning and an end. (source: 01_Project_Management_Introduction_Summary.md, section 1)
- A project should have a clear objective, a delivery date, and an understanding of the available resources or budget. (source: 01_Project_Management_Introduction_Summary.md, section 1)
- Project management assists an organization in assigning responsibility and authority, coordinating people, and converting a goal into action. (source: 01_Project_Management_Introduction_Summary.md, section 2)
- Projects can fail even when they seem to have a clear goal due to factors like technical problems, weak training, safety risks, logistics, and poor implementation. (source: 01_Project_Management_Introduction_Summary.md, section 2)
